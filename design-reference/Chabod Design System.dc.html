Design system

Built on Modernist's structure: square corners, 2px rules between sections and 1px rules between rows, flush-left labels, Lucide line icons. The colors come from the poster, muted, with dark as the default. The painted gradient appears only on the ካቦድ wordmark. Try it in the prototype.
