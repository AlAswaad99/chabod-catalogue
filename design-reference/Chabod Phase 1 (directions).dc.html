Chabod Choir Catalogue · Phase 1 · Turn 1

Three directions, one structure

Modernist's structure (square corners, 2px rules, flush-left labels, visible grid, line icons) set in Chabod's poster colors, muted, with dark as the default. The three directions differ in how the colors are assigned, the lyric typeface and where the search bar sits. Each direction shows a different login step (phone number, code, Telegram link), so all three steps get covered. The admin nav is the same in all three: Catalogue · Add · More, with More opening a slide-up sheet. Members don't see a bottom bar at all.
